Illinois issues a single combined "Salon/Shop Registration" covering cosmetology, esthetics, nail technology, hair braiding, and barbering under one certificate (68 Ill. Admin. Code 1175, Subpart M) — so a barber/salon split may not exist at the business level here, similar to what we found in Texas. "Shops" means business-level registrations only, not individual practitioner licenses, and excludes schools/continuing-education providers.
